# PACK: FINANCE (Health style, money heroes)

Money, saving, investing, banking, debt, credit, taxes, economics, business and tech-cost explainers. There is **no separate finance look**: the style is the Health pack's semi-realistic illustrated style exactly (the STYLE line of `template.md`, macro texture, light crosshatch on shadows only, clean faces and hands, rich saturated colors, one hero object filling at least half of each panel). Only the heroes change. Read `health.md` for the style, the People rules, the setting rule, the invisible-ideas approach and the safe-imagery rules; this file adds what Finance needs. Applies on top of `rules.md`, `layouts.md` and `template.md`.

## HEROES (decided from the cue's main noun)
Coins, bills, a wallet, a credit or debit card, a card reader, a piggy bank, a savings jar, a bank counter or vault door, a house key or house model, a calculator, a receipt or bill, an invoice, a contract, a loan document, a price tag, a shopping cart or bag, a paycheck envelope, a ledger book, a stock-market board seen as a physical board of plain bars or tiles, a delivery box, a server rack or a device for tech-cost cues. Drawn whole, large, in front and sharp, with macro texture (metal edges, reeded coin edges, paper fibres, plastic sheen). A person appears only when the cue is about a human experience (a worried shopper, a family at a table, a customer at a counter), with the hero object at a third of the frame.
- Abstract money ideas get a visible stand-in from the cue: **growth** = a taller stack, a bigger pile, a fuller jar across matching drawings; **debt or cost** = extra coins in one fixed deep red-copper color beside the original gold ones, clearly outnumbering them; **risk** = a person at the edge of a decision with the visible cost in the foreground; **inflation** = the same basket with fewer items for the same coins; **saving** = coins going into a jar or envelope over matching drawings; **interest** = a growing pile of deep red-copper (cost) or silver-green (earnings) coins beside the base stack, one color for the whole file. Never glowing dollar signs, floating icons, arrows, charts with axes, bags with a dollar symbol or candlestick charts.
- Scale follows the numbers: double the amount is about double the stack. One scale per panel.

## FIXED OBJECT LOOK (state in words in every canvas where the object appears)
Every coin is a round gold coin with a plain reeded edge and no writing; cost coins are the same coin in deep red-copper. A credit card is a deep navy rectangle with rounded corners, a glossy silver chip and one thin silver stripe, no writing, no logo. Bills are plain green-grey notes with no portrait, number or writing. A bank, shop or app is generic and unbranded. Recurring objects keep one fixed color, shape and size for the whole file.

## TEXT INSIDE PANELS
Same short exceptions as the Health pack, adapted: a single numeral, percentage or short amount the cue states as the point of the panel (for example a price tag, a calculator display or a round tile showing "5%") written as plain numerals on that carrier, one carrier per number, each number once, no currency symbol unless the cue's own words use it, no brand, no writing on cards, coins or bills. A contract, bill or invoice shows blank lines, never readable words.

## CHARACTERS
The main man is the narrator of "I" and "you" scripts, exactly as in the Health pack (reference image, exact line, outfit per scene). Every other person is created from the script, one short locked line each (for example "a woman in her late twenties with dark brown hair in a high ponytail, warm medium-tan skin, a soft blue shirt and plain cream trousers"), the same line word for word whenever that person appears; their face is shown only in panels that need it, and cropped hands (same skin tone every time) otherwise. A shopper, borrower, saver or small-business owner from a story is a locked character of this kind. Professionals (banker, accountant, advisor) are new characters with role props, never the main man unless the script says so.

## SETTING
Same script-only rule as the Health pack: with no place named, an ordinary modern American setting (shop, bank branch, kitchen table, home office, street); follow any named country or culture exactly. A cue about the past or another culture's money uses period-correct coins, tools and clothing and nothing modern.

## SAFE CLAIMS
No investment, tax or legal advice, no promise of returns, no guaranteed outcome the cue does not state. No real brands, banks, stock tickers, logos or currencies' real designs. A disclaimer cue ("not financial advice") uses the Health pack's disclaimer canvas (ribbon `DISCLAIMER`, one panel) with the notebook cover reading "NOT FINANCIAL ADVICE" and the panel title label "A QUICK NOTE" (the label never repeats the notebook text).

## PACK SELF-CHECK
Same style as Health; one money hero per panel at the right size; cost versus earnings colors are consistent for the whole file; no arrows, axes, dollar-sign symbols or glowing icons inside panels; coins, cards and bills carry no writing; numerals only on a single natural carrier; no real brand; no advice or promise added.

# PACK: BIBLE (cel-shaded, Christian)

Bible stories, character studies, sermons, parables, prophecy, doctrine, church history and devotionals. Applies on top of `rules.md`, `layouts.md` and `template.md`. STYLE uses the BIBLE alternatives. People of the Bible lands have Middle Eastern features and olive skin unless the script states another origin.

## TEXT PANELS (verse panels carry text inside comic canvases; heading and like-and-subscribe canvases are plain text slides, see items 2 and 3)
All text panels use one identical flat, full-panel sheet of warm cream parchment (soft even cream-tan, subtle paper-fiber grain, no scroll, no rods, no rolled or torn edges, no border, no shadow), with bold dark-brown Patrick Hand lettering, natural stroke wobble, centered in 1 to 3 lines with generous empty parchment on every side. No person, object, symbol, icon, drawing, seal, ornament or underline ever appears on one.
1. **Verse panel** (cue flagged VERSE): the cue's exact words, same spelling, numbers and punctuation, and nothing else. A reference at the END of the cue is its smaller last line; at the START the smaller first line. Every word of a verse appears in exactly one panel, in order, never moved between panels. Title label: always "SCRIPTURE" (a reference such as "JOHN 3:16" is already written on the parchment as its smaller line, so it is never repeated in the label). Description line: `n) Wide rectangle, title label "SCRIPTURE": a flat cream parchment sheet fills the whole panel with no picture; the text "(exact cue words)" is written in (1-3) centered lines in bold dark-brown Patrick Hand lettering. It is the only text and the only content of the panel.`
2. **Heading canvas** (`TEXT-CANVAS` in the plan): NOT a parchment panel and not a comic canvas. It is a plain text slide made with TEMPLATE B in `template.md`: pure white screen, the heading's exact words in black Patrick Hand lettering, nothing else (no frame, box, color, parchment or label).
3. **Like-and-subscribe canvas** (`TEXT-CANVAS` in the plan): NOT a parchment panel and not a comic canvas. It is a plain text slide made with TEMPLATE B in `template.md`: pure white screen with the words LIKE AND SUBSCRIBE in black Patrick Hand lettering, nothing else (no icon, button, box, color or label).
If a generator misspells panel text, the panel may be generated with "no text on the parchment" and the words added in an editor.

## SCENE PANEL KINDS (choose silently per cue)
Narrative scene (a Bible event as one full moment); cultural-context image (a custom, tool, place or daily-life detail shown in use); symbol or object image (drawn large and clear in its biblical setting); then-and-now (two matching views in one panel separated by a natural element, no frame lines, no text); modern-life scene (for "today" or "your life": a modern person in an ordinary modern place, locked like any character; the only kind not set in the biblical era); hook line (the strongest, most surprising visible moment); abstract line (through the visible stand-in). A closing question or call to the viewer gets one calm image that fits the lesson (a modern setting or a quiet landscape), never a text panel (a like/subscribe cue is the exception above).

## CHARACTER RULES
- **JESUS** (fixed, identical in every panel): early 30s, medium height, lean, slightly weathered from outdoor life, warm olive-brown skin, dark brown shoulder-length slightly wavy hair, short-to-medium natural dark brown beard, calm steady dark brown eyes, off-white linen inner tunic, muted blue-grey outer mantle, plain leather sandals. No halo, crown or glow unless the script states it. Clothing changes only where the script changes the situation; face, hair, beard and build never change. The same lock applies to Christ, Son of God, the Word and the Lord Jesus when the script is about Jesus on earth. A symbolic title that names an animal is drawn as that real animal.
- **GOD THE FATHER AND THE HOLY SPIRIT:** never drawn as a figure or a face. Shown only through the reactions of people (bowed heads, shielded eyes, raised faces), wind moving cloth and hair, or objects the script names (a burning bush, a cloud, a mountain, a scroll). When God speaks, the panel shows the listener or the scene, not a speaker. Do not link terms the script does not link; never decide doctrine for the script.
- **ANGELS AND HEAVENLY BEINGS:** identified silently, drawn in the standard look of Christian paintings. Identify by the script's name; if unnamed, from the event (a guardian of a sacred place is a cherub; a six-winged being at the throne is a seraph; a figure with a drawn sword confronting a traveler is the Angel of the LORD; a being delivering a message is a messenger angel; otherwise the plain messenger angel). Gabriel, Raphael and plain messenger angels: young male, long hair, long light robe, large white feathered wings. Michael: warrior angel in Roman-style armor with a sword, large white feathered wings. Angel of the LORD: tall male angel, white robe, large white wings, a drawn sword only where the script has one. Cherubim: large winged guardian beings (four-faced form in Ezekiel and throne scenes: man, lion, ox, eagle). Seraphim: six wings (two cover the face, two the feet, two spread). Heavenly host: a group of the same winged figures. No halo on any being. Any angel is male unless the script says otherwise. A being in more than one canvas is locked and repeated word for word.
- **ANIMALS:** the real animal, locked like a character. Visionary creatures (Daniel, Revelation) as the script or its chapter describes them.

## ERA
Setting, buildings, clothing, tools, food and objects match the period and region of each cue. No modern objects in biblical scenes. A modern-life scene uses modern clothing and settings.

## NUDITY AND VIOLENCE
Any biblical nudity is modest in every scene (back or side views, long hair, foliage, draped cloth, shadow), never explicit or sexualized. Death, crucifixion, battle and judgment with restraint: no gore, no graphic wounds, no blood pools; show the moment through posture, expression, light shapes and aftermath.

## TEXT POLICY
No text inside scene panels except on scrolls, books, parchments, stone inscriptions, decrees and temple records that are naturally in the scene (and only text the cue needs), and the text panels above.

## PACK SELF-CHECK
Every verse is a parchment text panel with exact text and all parchment panels look identical; every heading and like/subscribe canvas is a Template B plain white text slide; verse panels sit in the widest rows; the Jesus lock, the God/Spirit rule and the angel rule are followed; foreign words in narration are not written; nudity modest, violence restrained; no arrows, circles, ribbon or soft glow anywhere.

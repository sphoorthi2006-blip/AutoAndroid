# CANVAS PROMPT TEMPLATE - Bible and History

There are TWO prompt templates. TEMPLATE A (comic canvas) is used for every canvas that holds scene panels or verse panels. TEMPLATE B (plain text slide) is used for every heading canvas and every like-and-subscribe canvas, and for nothing else. Never mix them. Paste every block of the chosen template in full into every canvas prompt, with each {bracket} filled and each [IF ...] line kept or dropped as stated. Never shorten a block and never refer to an earlier prompt. Nothing outside the code block.

```
=== CANVAS {N}{ (paragraph P, part k of m) if the plan gives a part} ===
SCRIPT LINE (reference only, never drawn anywhere in the image; the numbers match the panel numbers): [1] "{cue text 1}" [2] "{cue text 2}" ...   (a one-cue canvas shows the single cue text without a number)
STYLE: a storyboard canvas on a plain pure white background with no paper grain and no tint, 16:9 image at the highest resolution available. The canvas furniture (panel outlines, labels, caption boxes) is flat and bold. There is no outer frame or border around the canvas. The artwork inside every panel is a 4K ultra-detailed 2D {BIBLE: biblical cinematic illustration | HISTORY: historical cinematic illustration of [ERA, YEAR, REGION AND CULTURE]} with semi-realistic characters, professional cel-shaded rendering with clean linework, expressive facial emotions, realistic anatomy, historically accurate clothing, architecture, tools and environment for the era and place, vibrant balanced colors, hard-edged cel shadow steps of two or three tones per surface, hard-edged light shapes, warm highlights and natural shadows, immersive depth, {BIBLE: Christian Bible story artwork | HISTORY: YouTube history documentary illustration style}. Light is drawn only as flat hard-edged shapes: no light rays, no haze, no glow, no lens flare, no gradients, no blur and no photographic texture; skies and water are flat bands of two or three tones. All shapes are closed and separated.
COLOR RULES: inside each panel use full natural vibrant colors suited to the scene, with a different dominant color in each panel so neighboring panels differ. Label and caption boxes are flat soft yellow #FFF1B8. The panel outlines are solid black. The white canvas background is never tinted.
FONT: every piece of text on the canvas (parchment and heading text, verse text, labels, caption boxes, balloons) is lettered in the Patrick Hand font: clean, casual, rounded handwriting with even stroke weight and open, friendly letterforms. No other typeface appears: no serif, gothic, calligraphy, script or ancient-style font.
NO OUTER BORDER (strict, always, never changes): the image itself is a pure white canvas filled edge to edge, and NOTHING is drawn around the whole set of panels. There is no outer rectangle, no outer frame, no border line, no box, no card, no outline and no shadow around the canvas or around the group of panels; the white background simply runs out to all four image edges. The panels float directly on the plain white canvas, each with its own thick black outline, with a generous even white margin between the panels and the image edges. The only black rectangles in the whole image are the outlines of the individual panels. Do not draw any line, rectangle or border along or near the image edges.
TITLE: none. This canvas has no ribbon, no banner and no title text. The canvas has no outer frame; the white canvas is used only for the panels, their labels and caption boxes.
CHARACTER RULES: consistent character design across every panel, and the same character looks identical in every panel where they appear. {the locked description of every character shown on this canvas, copied word for word from the locks, with the setting sentence: "The setting is (place and period). Nothing anachronistic appears."} {BIBLE: God is never drawn as a figure or a face.} {If no person appears: "No person appears in any panel."} No other person appears in any panel.
SCENES: every panel is its own complete scene with its own full background (ground, sky, walls or interior) that fills the panel edge to edge and is clipped by the panel outline. The subject is clearly separated from its background by contrast in color and brightness, and the background is simpler and softer in detail than the subject, with no text. Each panel's background is named in PANEL CONTENTS, and neighboring panels use different dominant background colors.
LAYOUT: Comic layout "{layout name}" with {n} rows and a {tight|standard|wide} gutter. {the geometry phrase from layouts.md, filled in} Layout rules that always apply:
{the six bullets of layouts.md section 6, with the Diagonal exception kept only for Diagonal layouts}
CONNECTIONS: none. There are no arrows, lines, paths or connectors of any kind on this canvas. Reading order is carried only by panel position, panel size and the caption boxes.
LABELS: two kinds, both filled with flat soft yellow.
(a) Title label: every panel has one torn-paper label box with a thick black outline, placed centered directly under its own panel, no wider than the panel, with bold black all-caps Patrick Hand lettering text of one to four words. The label touches its own panel: its top edge overlaps the panel's bottom outline by about a quarter of the label's height, so it reads as attached to that panel and never floats loose below it. The label never touches another panel, another label or the image edge, and has clear white space on both sides.
(b) Comic caption box: only on the panels listed in PANEL CONTENTS. A small rectangular narration box with a thick black outline, tucked inside the panel's top-left corner in empty space, with two to three words of bold black all-caps Patrick Hand lettering time or transition text. It never covers a character or object and never touches the panel outline.
[IF a balloon is used] (c) Speech balloon: only on the panels listed in PANEL CONTENTS. A plain white oval with a thick black outline and one small pointed tail toward the speaker, placed in the panel's empty space, holding the exact words listed in bold black Patrick Hand lettering. It never covers a face or the subject.
PANEL CONTENTS (reading order = cue order):
{one line per panel, written by rules.md section 7. Scene panel: `1) Rectangle, title label "…"[, caption box "…"][, balloon "…"]: {subject, action, posture, expression, camera}. Background: {named background}, dominant color {color}.` Say "Wide rectangle (the biggest panel)" for the key panel, and the position/size role for every panel (for example "Tall rectangle", "Small rectangle", "Parallelogram").}
{Text panels use the formats in the pack (verse, heading, call to subscribe).}
Each panel shows only what its description states, kept clear, and everything inside a panel stays within its outline.
SPACING AND TEXT: plenty of empty white space between panels, no overlapping panels, nothing breaking through a panel outline. {No text appears inside any panel. | [IF text panels] The only text inside any panel is the exact wording of the text panels listed in PANEL CONTENTS (and any in-world text the pack allows, listed in the panel).} All label text is perfectly spelled and exactly as given.
AVOID: a title label that repeats text already written inside its own panel, any font other than Patrick Hand, serif or ancient-style lettering, any outer frame, outer rectangle or border around the canvas or around the group of panels, a rectangle or line along the image edges, a double frame, a rounded outer border, a rounded white card or box around the canvas, a black or dark backdrop around the canvas, rounded canvas corners, a drop shadow around the panels, arrows, dashed lines, connector lines, circles, rounded corners, gradients, soft glow, light rays, haze, lens flare, blur, photographic look, 3D render, anime style, modern objects {BIBLE: halos, God drawn as a figure or face} , gore, {no text inside panels | text inside panels other than the listed text panels}, the script line or any cue text appearing anywhere in the image, misspelled text, garbled letters, extra words, extra characters, extra panels, number badges, {speech bubbles (drop this item if a balloon is used)}, watermark, logo, tinted or beige canvas background, paper grain, colored panel outlines, overlapping panels, panels spanning two rows (Tall layouts excepted), slanted or diagonal panel edges (drop for Diagonal layouts), inset panels, uneven gutters, panels of unequal height within one row, right-to-left rows, labels between two panels, extra caption boxes, caption boxes covering characters, ribbon banner, title banner, heading text, any lettering other than panel labels, caption boxes and listed text panels, extra people, a subject blending into its background.
```

Notes
- A History map panel may hold route or movement arrows on the map itself only; if one does, drop "arrows" from AVOID for that canvas and say so in the panel description. No other arrow is ever drawn.
- "modern objects" is dropped from AVOID for a modern-life History canvas; keep every other item.
- For a single-panel scene or verse canvas the LAYOUT line is Single splash, the label sits under the panel, and CONNECTIONS, LABELS and AVOID stay unchanged. The single panel has its own black outline and nothing around it. Heading and like-and-subscribe canvases do NOT use Template A at all; they use Template B below.
- The ERA bracket is filled once in STYLE. A panel that jumps to another era says so at the start of its own description and in its Background.
- Keep each prompt complete even when it is long; never drop a block to save space.

# TEMPLATE B - plain text slide (heading canvases and like-and-subscribe canvases only)

A text slide is a pure white screen with black text on it and nothing else: no frame, no border, no outline, no box, no panel, no parchment, no colored sheet, no icon, no button, no illustration, no label. Every text slide on a video looks identical; only the words change. Fill the one bracket and paste the block in full. Nothing outside the code block.

```
=== CANVAS {N} ===
A plain text slide, 16:9 image at the highest resolution available. The entire image is pure white #FFFFFF from edge to edge, with no tint, no paper grain, no texture and no gradient. The only thing on it is the text "{EXACT TEXT}" written in {1 or 2} centered line(s), centered horizontally and vertically, in large, bold, solid black Patrick Hand lettering (clean, casual, rounded handwriting with even stroke weight), with generous empty white space on every side. The text is perfectly spelled and exactly as given, and it is the only content of the image.
AVOID: any frame, border, outline, rectangle, box, panel, card, sheet, parchment, banner, ribbon, label, caption, colored background, colored text, tinted or beige or gray background, gradient, shadow, glow, texture, paper grain, underline, icon, button, symbol, drawing, illustration, person, object, decoration, any other text, extra words, misspelled text, garbled letters, any font other than Patrick Hand, serif or ancient-style lettering, watermark, logo.
```

- Heading cue: `{EXACT TEXT}` is the heading's exact words (for example `1. Ordered Words`), keeping the script's numbering, spelling and capitalization. One line when it fits in about 30 characters, otherwise two balanced lines.
- Like-and-subscribe cue: `{EXACT TEXT}` is `LIKE AND SUBSCRIBE` in one line. The cue's own words are never written.
- Template B has no SCRIPT LINE, no STYLE, no outer border line, no PANEL CONTENTS, no LABELS. A text slide has one panel in the JSON (empty label) but nothing in the image except the text.
